The Residence

Character out front. Polish inside.

158 sits within a small collection of blue character homes on a tree lined stretch of West 14th, with a gated walk, garden beds along the way in, and its own front door.

Inside, the renovating is already done. A white shaker kitchen with a gas range, stone mosaic tile and a full wall of pantry storage. Globe pendants over the dining table, wainscoting up the walls, a gas fireplace set in slate. A walk in closet fitted with built ins, and a stacked Bosch laundry pair in suite.

Both the primary and the den open through French doors to the outdoors, and the outdoors is what buyers will remember. Private, covered, planted. More on that below.

The Kitchen

Done properly, down to the pantry wall.

Shaker cabinetry, a stainless gas range under a chimney hood, stone mosaic tile and under cabinet task lighting. The pantry wall beside the fridge swallows a month of groceries, and the counter passes through to the dining room so the cook stays in the conversation.

The Main Room

Slate, firelight and globe glass.

A gas fireplace set in slate anchors the room, with the television niche built in above and pale wood floors running front to back. Crown moulding, wainscoting at the table, globe pendants overhead. It photographs calm because it is calm.

The Outdoor Rooms

Covered means January, not just July.

The den opens through French doors to a covered patio with a tongue and groove ceiling and cafe lights strung overhead. A second terrace holds the barbecue, planter boxes line the walk, and the primary bedroom has its own French doors out.

The Neighbourhood

Mount Pleasant, in the City Hall pocket.

West 14th here is a tree lined residential block between the Main Street and Cambie corridors, a few minutes on foot from either. City Hall and the Canada Line sit a short walk northwest, Mount Pleasant Park a couple of blocks east, and Simon Fraser Elementary one street over on West 15th. Genuinely quiet, genuinely central. Few pockets in the city manage both.
