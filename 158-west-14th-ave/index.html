01 — Overview

Character Out Front, Polish Inside

A rare garden townhome in an intimate four-home heritage conversion, tucked onto one of Mount Pleasant's prettiest tree-lined streets between Main and Cambie. The home lives entirely on one level, with a gated walkway, a private front door, and outdoor rooms on both sides, so daily life runs the way it does in a house.

Inside, the kitchen pairs a gas range and chimney hood with stone mosaic tile and a full wall of pantry storage. Wainscoting, crown moulding, globe pendants and a slate-surround gas fireplace give the main rooms character without feeling precious, and radiant in-floor heat runs throughout, so the wood floors are warm in January.

The primary bedroom opens through French doors to the garden and keeps a fitted walk-in closet. The den leads to a covered patio strung with café lights, and a second terrace gives the barbecue its proper place. The unglamorous things are done too: stacked Bosch laundry in suite, covered lane parking, shared storage, a new roof in 2021, and a self-managed four-home strata with low fees.

Your own front door on West 14th. Small strata, private garden, one very good address.

06 — Neighbourhood

The neighbourhood.

West 14th sits in the residential pocket between City Hall and Main Street, a few minutes on foot from either. The neighbourhood has been a village since 1888, and its centre at Main and Broadway holds thirteen buildings on the city's Heritage Register.

The Canada Line at Broadway-City Hall is a ten minute walk, and the Broadway Subway will add Mount Pleasant Station at Main and Broadway, with train testing underway as of spring 2026.
