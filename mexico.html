Three ways people do this.

Before you look at a single listing, get clear on what kind of Mexico life you actually want. Most of our clients land in one of these.

One

Sell everything and go.

Sell in BC, make Mexico home base, and put the difference to work. We plan the BC sale around the move, because selling before you leave is almost always simpler than selling from Mexico.

Two

Downsize and split the year.

Rightsize here, buy a condo there, and spend the grey months on the beach. You keep a home base near family, with the rules on MSP, vacancy and insurance mapped out before you go.

Three

Invest on the coast.

Buy a place you'll use some of the year and rent the rest. High season runs roughly November to April, and Bienvenidos manages rentals in-house, so the property earns while you're home.

Rent a season first.
Then buy.

A winter in town tells you more than any guide. We'll help you set one up, and make sure your BC home is sorted while you're away.

The Riviera Nayarit.

The coastline running north from Puerto Vallarta around the Bahía de Banderas, about five hours nonstop from YVR. This is where Bienvenidos works.

Bucerías

Cobblestone streets and a long sandy beach. Bienvenidos' home base.

La Cruz de Huanacaxtle

A working fishing village with a marina and a Sunday market.

Punta de Mita

The northern point of the bay. Gated communities, golf, and the top end of the market.

Sayulita

Surf town, colourful and busy. Strong for rentals, less so for quiet.

San Pancho

Sayulita's calmer neighbour, with a close-knit arts and community scene.

Nuevo Vallarta

Resort-style condos and wide beaches, closest to the airport.

Flamingos

Golf course living between Nuevo Vallarta and Bucerías.

Guayabitos

Further up the coast, a sheltered bay with a slower pace and lower entry prices.

How it works.

One plan for both ends of the move. We stay your point people in BC from the first call to the day you get your keys.

  1. A Mexico call

    Free, unhurried, no obligation. Your goals, your timeline, and which path fits.

  2. A plan for your BC home

    What it could sell for, whether to sell first or buy first, and the questions to take to your accountant before you decide.

  3. A season on the ground

    If you haven't spent a full season on the coast, rent first. We can help you find one.

  4. An introduction to Bienvenidos

    We connect you with the team in Bucerías for tours, listings and local advice.

  5. A safe purchase

    Deposit into third-party escrow, a notario you trust, a fideicomiso set up properly, and title insurance. Never a wire to anyone directly.

  6. Keys, and someone to watch the place

    Rentals and property management through Bienvenidos, so the home is cared for while you're in BC.
