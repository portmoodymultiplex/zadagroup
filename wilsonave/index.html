Step out the front door and into the trees.

Orchid sits at the trailhead of the Traboulay PoCo Trail, right beside Gates Park and minutes from the Coquitlam River. Morning runs, evening walks and weekend rides all start at your door.

It is also one of the most walkable addresses in Port Coquitlam. The PoCo Rec Centre, downtown shops, restaurants and breweries are all within easy reach, and the West Coast Express is a short walk away.

Inside, residents enjoy two fitness centres, a sauna, a library, a games room, a private guest suite and landscaped gardens.
