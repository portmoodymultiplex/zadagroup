Port Moody Real Estate

Port Moody, from the team that lives here.

Houses, townhomes, condos, and multiplex development across Port Moody and the Tri-Cities.

Port Moody is a small city with an unusual amount going on: two SkyTrain stations, a working waterfront at Rocky Point, Brewers Row, and some of the most varied housing stock in Metro Vancouver, from 1960s ranchers in Glenayre to new towers rising at Coronation Park. It is also where we live. Zada Group is a boutique team led by Dawar Zada, PREC, a Master Medallion Club REALTOR® licensed with Stonehaus Realty Corp, and our practice is built on repeat clients and referrals, not volume.

If you are buying or selling in Port Moody, the local details matter more than they would in a bigger market. Which streets back onto the inlet trail. Which complexes have upcoming levies. Which lots quietly gained multiplex potential when the SSMUH rules came in. That last one is our specialty: Dawar was the first homeowner in Port Moody to move a multiplex application forward under the new framework, on our own property, so we know the process from the applicant's side, not just the brochure.

Port Moody neighbourhoods.

Moody Centre

The historic heart. Character homes and new development side by side, walkable to the SkyTrain, Rocky Point Park, and Brewers Row. This is also where SSMUH and transit-oriented zoning are changing what lots are worth.

Heritage Woods & Heritage Mountain

Newer family homes up the mountain, strong schools including Heritage Woods Secondary, and greenbelt on your doorstep. The default answer when buyers ask us where families land.

Klahanie, Suter Brook & Newport Village

Master-planned townhome and condo communities around Inlet Centre station. Walk to groceries, the rec centre, and the waterfront. Popular with first-time buyers and downsizers alike.

College Park & Glenayre

Established 1960s neighbourhoods on the Burnaby border, minutes to SFU. Bigger lots and solid ranchers, and many of them are exactly the kind of underbuilt properties the new multiplex rules were written for.

Pleasantside & Ioco

The quiet north shore of the inlet, near Old Orchard Park and the Ioco townsite. Larger properties, mature trees, and a pace that feels further from the city than it is.

Coronation Park

A neighbourhood in transition, with major redevelopment approved around Inlet Centre station. If you own here or are watching it, timing and information are everything. We follow it closely.

Common questions.

Are you actually based in Port Moody?

Yes. We live and work in the Tri-Cities, our kids go to school here, and Port Moody is the core of our practice.

Which neighbourhoods are best for families?

Heritage Woods and Heritage Mountain for newer homes and schools, College Park and Glenayre for bigger lots near SFU, Klahanie and Suter Brook for walkable townhome living. The right answer depends on budget, schools, and commute, and we are happy to talk it through.

Can I build a multiplex on my lot?

Under SSMUH, most single-family lots in Port Moody can now support multiple units, but lot size, servicing, and access decide what actually works. Ask us for a no-pressure assessment.

How do I find out what my home is worth?

Contact us for an evaluation built from recent comparable sales in your specific neighbourhood. If your lot has multiplex potential, we will tell you what that adds.

Do you cover Coquitlam and Port Coquitlam too?

Yes. Our practice covers the Tri-Cities plus Anmore and Belcarra. Port Moody is home base.
