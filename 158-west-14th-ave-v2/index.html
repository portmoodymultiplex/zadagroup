158 West 14th Avenue Mount Pleasant, Vancouver

Scroll

A renovated garden townhome on one of Mount Pleasant's quietest blocks.

158 West 14th is a ground level home in a four home heritage conversion, on a tree lined avenue between Main Street and Cambie. Three bedrooms, two bathrooms and 1,127 square feet on a single level, with a covered patio off the den, a second terrace for the barbecue, and French doors from both the den and the primary bedroom. The renovation is finished. There is nothing left to do but move in.

The kitchen was redone with white shaker cabinetry, a gas range under a chimney hood, stone mosaic tile and a full wall of pantry storage beside the fridge. The living and dining rooms run front to back under globe pendants, with wainscoting, crown moulding and a gas fireplace set in slate.

Radiant floor heat runs through the whole home, fed by an on demand boiler. The laundry is a stacked Bosch pair inside the suite. Outside there is a covered parking space off the lane and shared storage, and the roof was replaced in 2021.

The kitchen.

White shaker cabinetry, a stainless gas range under a chimney hood, stone mosaic tile and task lighting under the cabinets. The pantry wall beside the fridge holds more than most kitchens twice this size, and the counter opens through to the dining room.

The main room.

A gas fireplace set in slate, with the television built in above it and pale wood floors running the length of the home. Crown moulding, wainscoting at the dining table and globe pendants overhead. It is a calm room, and the light from the front windows carries all the way through.

The outdoor rooms.

The den opens through French doors onto a covered patio with a tongue and groove ceiling and cafe lights, usable through most of the year. A second terrace holds the barbecue, planter boxes line the walk in, and the primary bedroom has its own doors to the garden.

Mount Pleasant.

West 14th sits in the residential pocket between City Hall and Main Street, a few minutes on foot from either. The neighbourhood has been a village since 1888, and its centre at Main and Broadway holds thirteen buildings on the city's Heritage Register.

The Canada Line at Broadway-City Hall is a ten minute walk. The Broadway Subway will add Mount Pleasant Station at Main and Broadway, with train testing underway as of spring 2026. Simon Fraser Elementary is one street over on West 15th, and Mount Pleasant Park is two blocks east.
