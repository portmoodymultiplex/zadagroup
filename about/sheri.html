The job

Two things, done every day

Sheri runs communications and community relations for Zada Group, the Port Moody real estate team she built with her husband, Dawar Zada, PREC*.

In practice that means two things. She makes sure the team says something worth hearing in public. And she makes sure our clients never get blindsided by a decision made at City Hall or in Victoria.

Why it matters

A 25 year decision, made under rules that change every four

Councils turn over. Governments change. Zoning gets rewritten. Most buyers find out what that means for their street after they have already bought on it.

We don't think that's good enough. Between the market, the legislation, the municipalities and the government agencies behind them, there is a whole picture, and our clients should have all of it before they sign. That is the edge Sheri brings to the team: real estate as one connected plan, not a transaction followed by four years of hoping.

My job is making brilliant, busy people legible to the outside world.

Sheri Zada

What she isn't

Sheri is not a licensed REALTOR® and does not trade in real estate.

When you are ready to buy or sell, you'll work with Dawar or one of our licensed agents. When you want to know what a new council, a new provincial government or a new zoning rule means for your street, you'll talk to Sheri. Most of our clients end up doing both.
