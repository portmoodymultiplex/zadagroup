Just Listed / Just Sold, on demand

These are the fill-in-the-blank versions the whole team can use. The workflow: whoever has the deal drops the listing sheet (or just the address, price, and one detail worth mentioning) into the shared Dropbox folder or straight into a Claude session, says "just listed" or "just sold," and gets the finished caption back. Nobody writes from scratch again.

On the market. [ADDRESS], [NEIGHBOURHOOD], [CITY].

Template rules, so every post sounds like us: numbers over adjectives, always. No "stunning," "dream home," "must-see," "luxurious," or "act now." No em dashes. Canadian spelling. One exclamation mark maximum, and only on celebration posts. Hashtags lowercase. Every post names the agent, Zada Group, and Stonehaus Realty Corp.

The Dropbox trigger, phased honestly

Phase 1 (works today): the team drops a listing sheet or MLS data sheet into the Dropbox folder, then whoever's posting opens a Claude session, attaches it, and says "just listed" or "just sold." The templates above plus your voice rules produce the finished caption in one pass. Total human effort: one drag, two words.

Phase 2 (worth building once posting is habitual): a Make.com or Zapier scenario watches two Dropbox folders, "01 Just Listed" and "02 Just Sold." A new file triggers a draft: the automation reads the sheet, fills the template, and creates a scheduled draft in Meta Business Suite via the Facebook Pages connection. You approve drafts in the Planner rather than writing anything.

My suggestion: run Phase 1 for a month. If the team actually uses the folders, Phase 2 is a one-afternoon build and I can spec the whole scenario with you.
